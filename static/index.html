About AZ PDF LOVER

AZ PDF LOVER is a lightweight collection of online PDF tools designed to make everyday document tasks easier. Whether you are preparing schoolwork, organizing business paperwork, or converting a report, the goal is to offer clear tools in one accessible place without requiring desktop software.

Why PDF tools matter

PDFs preserve document layout across devices, but editing or combining them can be inconvenient. Browser-based utilities help with common tasks while keeping the workflow simple. Wherever possible, this page processes your files directly in your browser instead of uploading them to a document-processing server.

Popular PDF features

  • Merge PDF: combine multiple PDF files into a single document.
  • Split and extract: select page ranges or create a separate file for chosen pages.
  • Compress PDF: attempt safe structural re-saving; this cannot reliably recompress embedded images or fonts in every PDF.
  • Convert PDF: render pages to JPG, extract available text to DOCX-compatible content, or export extracted text/tables to spreadsheets.
  • Organize, rotate and delete: rearrange or remove pages and adjust page orientation.
  • Sign and watermark: place a drawn signature or text watermark onto selected pages.

Privacy and security

🔒
Local-first processing
Selected documents are not intentionally uploaded by this app. Processing happens in your browser. The site does load its JavaScript libraries from CDNs; once loaded, your document bytes are handled locally. For highly confidential files, use a trusted offline copy of the app and verify the code before use.

Encrypted PDFs may require a password or may be unsupported. This page does not bypass document permissions. Browser libraries cannot perfectly preserve every font, layout, form, attachment, or complex feature. OCR requires downloading language data from the OCR library's configured source.
